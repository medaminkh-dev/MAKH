## When the AGPL is the right choice

Research, teaching, hobby projects, open-source products, and anyone happy to
publish their modifications. The AGPL's network clause (§13) means that if you
run a modified MakhOS and let people interact with it over a network, you must
offer them the source of your modified version.

## When you need the commercial license

- You embed MakhOS (or a derivative) in a proprietary device, appliance or
  product and do not want to release your source code.
- You run a modified MakhOS as a hosted/cloud/managed service and want to keep
  your modifications private.
- Your organisation's policy does not allow AGPL software.
- You want a warranty, indemnification, guaranteed support or custom work.
